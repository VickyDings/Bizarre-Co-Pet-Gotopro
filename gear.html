What we'd actually

Humidity gear is where new keepers overspend and still get it wrong. Three items do the whole job — and we'll tell you which one to skip.

Best overall

Digital hygrometer with remote probe

Analogue dial gauges drift badly within months. A probe you can place at branch height is the highest-value twenty dollars here.

±3% RH · 5 ft probe · min/max memory

Best value

Ultrasonic fogger, three litre

Run it on a night-only timer. Continuous fogging is the mistake that turns a healthy enclosure into a respiratory-infection risk.

Timer 15 min–24 h · adjustable output

Skip this

Heat mats sold as “humidity boosters”

They dry the substrate and cook it from below. Crested geckos are an ambient-temperature species — wrong tool entirely.

Under-tank · adhesive · no thermostat

How we test

The method,
in three steps.

No product enters a list because a brand asked. Every pick starts from the husbandry requirement, not the catalogue.

01

Start from the number

Before looking at products we write down what the animal needs — the UVI, the RH range, the gallons, the wattage.

02

Test against it

Gear is measured against that figure with independent instruments, in a real enclosure, over weeks rather than an afternoon.

03

Publish the failures

Every list names at least one popular product we think you should avoid, and says exactly why.
