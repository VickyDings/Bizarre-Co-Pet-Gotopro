Almost every aquarium disaster starts the same way: someone stands in front of a wall of tanks, likes the look of six different fish, and buys them. A fortnight later the guppies have shredded fins, the shrimp have vanished, and the "algae eater" has grown to the size of a shoe.

Fish compatibility isn't about which species look nice together. It's about temperament, adult size, water temperature, swimming level, and whether one animal considers the other food. The chart below covers the 22 groups you'll actually find in a US pet shop, and every pairing in it has been cross-checked so it never contradicts itself.
