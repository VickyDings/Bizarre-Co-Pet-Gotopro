5 Hydration Tips Every
Cat Owner Should Know

Good hydration supports digestion, immunity and energy — hydrated cat, happy cat!
1

💧Fresh Water Every Day

Change water 2–3 times daily and wash the bowl itself — clean, fresh-tasting water is the single easiest way to encourage cats to drink more.

2

🥫Feed Wet Food Regularly

Wet food is roughly 75% moisture and does the hydration heavy-lifting, helping maintain healthy kidneys and urinary flow from the inside.

3

🍪Don't Rely on Dry Food Alone

Kibble is only ~10% moisture. A dry-only diet can quietly lead to chronic dehydration — balance is the key.

4

⛲Use a Pet Water Fountain

Moving, filtered water attracts cats instinctively and encourages them to drink noticeably more — see our tested fountain picks below.

5

❄️Keep Them Cool

Heat drives water loss. Keep your cat's resting spots cool and offer extra water stations in summer, especially for kittens and seniors.

⚠️ Watch for Signs of Dehydration

  • Dry or sticky gums
  • Sunken eyes
  • Low energy or lethargy
  • Loss of appetite
  • Skin stays "tented" when gently lifted
  • Concentrated, strong-smelling urine
Notice any of these? Offer fresh water and contact your vet if it doesn't improve quickly.
