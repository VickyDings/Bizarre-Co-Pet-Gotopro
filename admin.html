    Step-by-step guide
    
                    
    Part 1 is a one-time setup. Part 2 onward is what you do for every article.
                       Each step says exactly where in the console to click.
    
                
    

                
    
                    
                    
    Want to see it work before reading any of this?
                        Go to the composer, press Load a sample post, then
                        Publish & share. You will see the whole flow end to end with a
                        real bearded dragon care guide, and nothing is posted anywhere until you click a
                        network yourself.
    
                
    

                
                
    
                    
    Part 1 — First-time setup about 5 minutes, done once
    
                    
      
                        
    1. 
                            Settings  Site
                            Put in your live site URL
                            
      Type the address your blog will actually live at, for example
                               https://bizarreco.com — no trailing slash.
      
                            Why it matters: every share link, every tracked
                               URL and the canonical tag are built from this. Leave it blank and your captions
                               go out with no link in them.
                        
      2. 
                        
    2. 
                            Settings  Amazon Associates
                            Add your Associate tag
                            
      The tag looks like yourname-20. You get it from your Amazon
                               Associates dashboard.
      
                            Why it matters: without it the product links the
                               assistant generates still work, but earn you nothing. The SEO panel will warn you
                               in red until it is set.
                        
      3. 
                        
    3. 
                            Settings  Campaign tracking
                            Leave UTM on the defaults
                            
      utm_medium of social is right for almost everyone.
                               Leave utm_campaign blank and each post uses its own slug.
      
                            Why it matters: this is how your analytics tells
                               Pinterest traffic from Instagram traffic. Without it everything lands in one
                               undifferentiated "social" bucket.
                        
      4. 
                        
    4. 
                            Social SEO setup
                            Switch off the networks you do not use
                            
      Thirteen networks are on by default. Toggle off anything you have no account
                               for — you will not have to skip past it on every publish.
      
                            
      For the ones you keep, fill in your handle and profile URL. The
                               Extra default tags box is for a brand tag you want on every
                               post, like #bizarreco.
      
                        
      5. 
                        
    5. 
                            Social SEO setup  top of page
                            Set your brand name and default call to action
                            
      The call to action is the closing line of every caption. Something like
                               “Full care guide on the blog” works.
      
                        
      6. 
                        
    6. 
                            Settings  Fan-out webhook
                            Optional: set up true simultaneous posting
                            
      If you want posts going out to every network without you touching each
                               composer, make a catch hook in Zapier, Make or Buffer and paste the URL here.
                               Press Send a test payload to confirm it arrives, then
                               Show the payload shape so you know what fields to map.
      
                            This is the only hands-off route. Skip it and
                               publishing still works — you just click through the networks yourself,
                               which takes about a minute.
                        
      7. 
                        
    7. 
                            Settings  Console passcode
                            Optional: set a passcode
                            
      Keeps the console out of the way if someone else uses your screen.
      
                            Not security. It is checked in the browser, so
                               anyone who can read the page source can read it. Before this site goes live,
                               put real access control in front of admin.html — HTTP basic
                               auth or a host access rule.
                        
      8. 
                        
    8. 
                            Press Save settings
                            
      Everything is stored in this browser only. Setup is now done forever
                               — from here on you go straight to Part 2.
      
                        
      9. 
                    
    
                
    

                
                
    
                    
    Part 2 — Write the article every post
    
                    
      
                        
    1. 
                            Post composer  Headline
                            Write the headline
                            
      Aim for 30–60 characters, with the thing people search for in the first
                               half. The counter beside the label turns green when you are in range.
      
                            Good: “Bearded Dragon Basking Temperature:
                               Getting UVB and Heat Right”
      
                               Weak: “Some thoughts on lizard lighting” —
                               nobody searches that.      
                            
      Stuck? Put in any rough headline, run the analysis in step 10, and the
                               Headline angles card gives you ten proven shapes filled in with
                               your species. Click one to use it.
      
                        
      2. 
                        
    2. 
                            Post composer  Animal group
                            Pick the animal group
                            
      This is the most important dropdown on the page. It selects the entire keyword
                               corpus — the seed terms, the husbandry vocabulary, the hashtag sets, the
                               product pairings and the health problems used for your questions.
      
                            Get this wrong and everything downstream is wrong.
                               A hermit crab post filed under Aquatics will suggest filters and water
                               parameters instead of shell shops and moulting.
                        
      3. 
                        
    3. 
                            Post composer  Species focus
                            Leave species blank unless you need to force it
                            
      The assistant reads your text and detects the species itself. Only type here if
                               your draft mentions several animals and you want the suggestions pinned to one.
                               The box suggests valid options as you type.
      
                        
      4. 
                        
    4. 
                            Post composer  URL slug
                            Leave the slug blank
                            
      The assistant builds a short, keyphrase-led slug from your headline and strips
                               the filler words. Only override it if you have an existing URL to match.
      
                        
      5. 
                        
    5. 
                            Post composer  Excerpt
                            Write the excerpt — this one earns its keep
                            
      One or two sentences, 80–200 characters. State the problem the article
                               solves, not what the article contains.
      
                            It gets used three times: as your meta
                               description in Google, as the opening line of every social caption, and as the
                               bold lead paragraph of the published post. It is the single highest-leverage
                               field on this page.
                            Good: “Most bearded dragon health problems
                               trace back to one number on a thermometer.”
                        
      6. 
                        
    6. 
                            Post composer  Article body
                            Write the body, using ## for subheadings
                            
      Start a line with ## to make a subheading and - to
                               make a bullet. That is the whole formatting syntax.
      
                            
      The word counter beside the label goes green at 900 words. Care guides that
                               rank in this niche usually run 1,200–2,000.
      
                            Why the ## matters: each subheading is a chance
                               at a featured snippet. The score panel counts them, and step 14 can generate
                               the whole outline for you.
                        
      7. 
                        
    7. 
                            Post composer  Featured image
                            Paste an image URL, then press Suggest alt text
                            
      1200×630 is the size for link cards on Facebook, X and LinkedIn. For
                               Pinterest you also want a tall 1000×1500 version.
      
                            
      The Suggest alt text button writes a description from your
                               species and keyphrase. Edit it so it describes what is actually in the photo
                               — alt text is for people using screen readers first, search engines second.
      
                        
      8. 
                        
    8. 
                            Post composer  Author & trust signals
                            Fill in both the author and the reviewer
                            
      Pet health is what search engines call a YMYL topic — “your money or
                               your life”. Pages that can affect an animal's wellbeing are held to a
                               higher bar on who wrote them.
      
                            
      The reviewer can be an exotics vet, a long-time keeper, a breeder or a groomer.
                               Put their actual credential: “Dr. A. Patel BVSc, exotics”, not
                               “our team”.
      
                            Both names go into the published page's structured data as the
                               author and reviewedBy fields, which is where search engines look
                               for them. Leaving them blank costs you points on the score.
                        
      9. 
                        
    9. 
                            Post composer  Search intent
                            Pick which intents to target
                            
      The three on by default are right for a care guide. Add
                               Transactional and Local only for shop pages
                               — those phrases belong on a product page, not a blog post, and the table
                               will tell you so in the “Use as” column.
      
                        
      10. 
                        
    10. 
                            Press Run AI SEO analysis
                            
      The big purple button at the bottom of the composer. It takes you straight to
                               the next part.
      
                        
      11. 
                    
    
                
    

                
                
    
                    
    Part 3 — Work the score up the part that actually moves rankings
    
                    
      
                        
    1. 
                            AI SEO assistant  On-page score
                            Read the failing checks, top down
                            
      Failed checks sort to the top and each one tells you the specific fix. Work
                               down the list. Word count, keyphrase in the title and keyphrase in the
                               opening lines carry the most weight — fix those first.
      
                            
      Aim for 85 or above before publishing. Below 50 the post is not ready.
      
                        
      2. 
                        
    2. 
                            AI SEO assistant  Focus keyphrase
                            Confirm or swap the focus keyphrase
                            
      This is the one phrase the page should own. The assistant picks the best
                               candidate from your draft; the chips below are the alternatives, with how many
                               times each appears.
      
                            
      Click a chip to switch and the whole analysis re-runs instantly. Prefer a
                               two or three word phrase that names the topic, not the animal —
                               “basking temperature” beats “bearded dragon”, because
                               thousands of pages are about bearded dragons.
      
                        
      3. 
                        
    3. 
                            AI SEO assistant  Semantic gaps
                            Click the gaps that genuinely belong in your article
                            
      These are terms search engines expect on a page about this topic that your
                               draft does not mention. Clicking one appends it to the end of your body as a
                               TODO cover: note.
      
                            
      Then go back to the composer and actually write those sections,
                               deleting the TODO lines as you go. Leaving the notes in place helps nothing
                               — the point is the coverage, not the words.
      
                            Skip any gap that does not fit your article. A post about
                               moulting does not need to cover brackish water just because the term exists in
                               the corpus.
                        
      4. 
                        
    4. 
                            AI SEO assistant  Questions to answer
                            Press Insert as H2 outline
                            
      This appends every question to your body as a subheading with an
                               “ANSWER in 40–60 words” placeholder. Answer them in your own
                               words, delete the ones that do not fit, and delete every placeholder you did
                               not answer.
      
                            Important: these questions also become the FAQ
                               structured data in the published file. Shipping the page with placeholder
                               answers still in it breaches Google's structured-data policy and can get the
                               rich result pulled. Answer them or remove them.
                        
      5. 
                        
    5. 
                            AI SEO assistant  Long-tail keyword set
                            Skim the keyphrase table and use the “Use as” column
                            
      Each row tells you where the phrase belongs. “H2 inside this post”
                               means work it into a subheading here. “Shop page, not the blog”
                               means leave it alone for this article.
      
                            About the competition labels: they are
                               calculated from phrase length and specificity, not live search volume. Treat
                               them as a shortlist to check in a proper keyword tool, not a forecast.
                        
      6. 
                        
    6. 
                            AI SEO assistant  Amazon product pairings
                            Pick two or three products and put them in the article
                            
      Copy HTML gives you a ready link with
                               rel="nofollow sponsored" already on it. Paste it into your body
                               where the product is genuinely the answer to a problem.
      
                            
      These are category search links. Once you have tested a specific product, swap
                               the link for that product's own page.
      
                            Amazon's terms require a visible affiliate disclosure
                               on any page carrying these links. The published file includes one automatically
                               at the bottom. The same terms forbid putting these links in emails or PDFs
                               — so do not paste them into your newsletter.
                        
      7. 
                        
    7. 
                            Search & social preview
                            Check how it looks before anyone else does
                            
      You see the Google result and the shared link card exactly as they will appear.
                               The meta title and description are editable right here — rewrite them to
                               earn the click rather than just describe the page.
      
                            
      Keep the title under 60 characters and the description between 120 and 155, or
                               Google truncates them mid-word.
      
                        
      8. 
                        
    8. 
                            Re-run the analysis and check the score again
                            
      Go back to the composer and press Run AI SEO analysis. Repeat
                               Part 3 until the score is where you want it.
      
                        
      9. 
                    
    
                
    

                
                
    
                    
    Part 4 — Publish and share about a minute
    
                    
      
                        
    1. 
                            Top bar  Publish & share
                            Press Publish & share
                            
      Two things happen: the post is saved to your content library, and the
                               multi-share window opens with a caption ready for every enabled network.
      
                            
      Nothing is posted anywhere yet. Nothing leaves this page until
                               you click one of the buttons below.
      
                        
      2. 
                        
    2. 
                            Multi-share  Download post HTML
                            Download the post file and commit it
                            
      Bottom-left of the share window. You get a complete HTML file with the meta
                               tags, Open Graph tags, Twitter card and JSON-LD already in the head.
      
                            
      Put it in a blog/ folder in your repository and commit. That file
                               is the published article — the share links point at it, so put it
                               live before or shortly after you share.
      
                        
      3. 
                        
    3. 
                            Multi-share  caption cards
                            Read the captions and edit anything that reads oddly
                            
      Every card is editable with a live character count. A red count means you are
                               over that network's limit. The X card already accounts for links counting as
                               23 characters regardless of their real length.
      
                            
      Toggle off any network you do not want this particular post going to.
      
                        
      4. 
                        
    4. 
                            Multi-share  Open all intent networks
                            Press Open all intent networks
                            
      Ten networks open in one click, each with the caption and tracked link already
                               filled in where that network allows it.
      
                            
      If your popup blocker stops some, they are listed underneath with their own
                               button — nothing is dropped silently. Allowing popups for this page makes
                               the whole set open together next time.
      
                            Facebook and LinkedIn will need a paste. Both
                               strip pre-filled text from their share dialogs, so use the
                               Copy button on those two cards and paste into the dialog that
                               opens.
                        
      5. 
                        
    5. 
                            Multi-share  Start compose stepper
                            Press Start compose stepper for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube
                            
      These three have no public pre-fill URL, so they cannot be opened pre-filled by
                               any tool. The stepper handles them one at a time: it copies the caption, opens
                               the composer, and moves to the next when you come back.
      
                            Why not automatic? There is no legitimate way to
                               post to these from a web page. Anything claiming to do it is either riding your
                               logged-in session, which breaks their terms and can get you banned, or using
                               their official API behind OAuth and app review. The webhook in step 24 is the
                               proper route.
                        
      6. 
                        
    6. 
                            Multi-share  Fire fan-out webhook
                            Or, if you set up a webhook, just press that instead
                            
      One click sends the post, every caption, every hashtag set and every tracked URL
                               to your automation, which publishes to all connected profiles at once. This
                               replaces steps 22 and 23 entirely.
      
                        
      7. 
                    
    
                
    

                
                
    
                    
    Part 5 — Afterwards where the compounding happens
    
                    
      
                        
    1. 
                            Best times & cadence
                            Work the repost ladder
                            
      One article is worth eight posts over three months. The table there lays out
                               what goes where on day 0, 1, 2, 4, 7, 21 and 90.
      
                            
      The day 90 row matters most: refresh the post, update the year, re-share.
                               Refreshed pages outrank abandoned ones.
      
                        
      2. 
                        
    2. 
                            Content library  Re-share
                            Use Re-share for every later touch
                            
      Re-share rebuilds the captions from the saved post and opens the share window
                               again. Edit the caption to a different angle each time — posting the same
                               words repeatedly gets throttled on most networks.
      
                        
      3. 
                        
    3. 
                            Content library  Export library
                            Export the library every so often
                            
      Everything lives in this browser. Clearing site data or switching device loses
                               it. Export the JSON, or rely on the committed post files, to keep anything that
                               matters.
      
                        
      4. 
                    
    
                
    

                
    
                    
     Honest limits, in one place
    
                    
      
                        
    • Browsers cannot post to Instagram, TikTok or YouTube from a page like this.
                            Those platforms have no public pre-fill URL. The webhook route in Settings is the
                            legitimate hands-off path.
      • 
                        
    • Popup blockers cap how many tabs open at once. The share window opens
                            every intent network in one click and then lists any the browser blocked with its own
                            button, so nothing is silently dropped.
      • 
                        
    • Competition labels are heuristics based on phrase length and specificity.
                            They are a shortlist to check in a real keyword tool, not search volume.
      • 
                        
    • The posting windows are general pet-niche guidance, not your analytics.
                            Once you have 30 days of data in each platform's insights, trust that instead.
      • 
                        
    • Everything is stored in this browser (localStorage). Export the library
                            or commit the downloaded files to keep your work.
      • 
                        
    • The passcode is not security. Put HTTP auth, a host access rule or a real
                            login in front of admin.html before this site goes live.
      • 
                        
    • The assistant is a keyword engine, not a writer. It will not write the
                            article, and it cannot tell you whether your husbandry advice is correct. That part is
                            still yours.
