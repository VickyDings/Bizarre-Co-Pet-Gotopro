Could this be passed to whoever manages your CJ Affiliate program or your

I am an approved affiliate for Raw Paws Pet Food through CJ, publishing at
pet-gotopro.com (CJ advertiser 7127091, publisher property 101894827). I have
been building product recommendations from your feed and found two faults that
affect all 158 products. Neither looks specific to my account.


1. Every destination URL contains two spaces

The feed returns product URLs in this form:

    https://www.rawpawspetfood.com /products/ chicken-necks-for-dogs-2-lb
                                  ^          ^

One space after the domain, a second after /products/. It is identical on every
one of the 158 products, so it reads as a template rather than individual bad
records.

A space is not valid in a URL. CJ encodes it faithfully, the browser receives
%20 in both positions, and the page does not load. I checked this two ways:
through the Product Feed API, and through CJ's own link generator in the member
area, which writes the same two spaces into the form it builds.

The practical effect is that a tracking link built from your feed today does not
reach your store, so any affiliate traffic generated from it is lost.


2. The product feed carries no images

Both image fields are present but empty across all 158 products:

    imageLink            empty
    additionalImageLink  empty

CJ's link generator reflects the same thing - the image creative it builds for a
product comes out as an img tag with src="null".

Your program terms ask publishers to use only images supplied through the CJ
Account Manager. The banner creatives there do work, and I am using two of them.
But with the feed empty there is no approved image for an individual product, so
a product recommendation has to run without a photo. In my experience that costs
a meaningful share of the clicks.


Everything else arrives correctly - titles, descriptions, prices, availability
and SKUs are all fine, so this looks like two narrow faults rather than anything
broad.

I would be glad to confirm a fix once it is deployed. I can re-pull the feed and
check both items the same day.
