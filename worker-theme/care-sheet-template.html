💚 Signs of a Healthy Budgie

  • Bright, clear eyes and a smooth, dry cere
  • Sleek feathers held close, not fluffed all day
  • Active, chirping, chewing and climbing
  • Hulled seed husks — not whole seed on the floor
  • Clean vent, firm well-formed droppings
  • Steady weight week to week on a gram scale

⚠️ Call an Avian Vet If

  • Tail bobbing with each breath — same-day emergency
  • Sitting fluffed on the cage floor — same-day emergency
  • Whole unhulled seed left on the cage floor
  • Droppings change colour or stay runny past a day
  • Clicking or wheezing while breathing
  • Weight down more than 10%, or crusty cere or legs

The 4 Rules That Prevent Almost Every Problem

1

Bar spacing ½ inch or less. Wider bars let a budgie push its head through and not pull it back — a fatal entrapment that takes minutes.

2

Bin the dowel perches that came with the cage. One diameter, gripped the same way all day, causes pressure sores and then bumblefoot. Use natural wood in varied widths.

3

Never heat non-stick near a bird. PTFE fumes from pans, air fryers, self-cleaning ovens and some heaters kill within 30 minutes, with no treatment.

4

Seed is a treat, not a diet. All-seed feeding causes obesity, fatty liver disease and iodine deficiency — and roughly halves a budgie's lifespan.
