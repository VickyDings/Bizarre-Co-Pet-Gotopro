About Bizarre Co

The story behind the most unique store you'll ever visit

Our Story

Where Passion Meets the Extraordinary

Founded in 2020, Bizarre Co was born from a simple idea: create a space where collectors and animal lovers could find the extraordinary under one roof.

What started as a small hobby shop has grown into a thriving community of passionate collectors, dedicated pet owners, and curious explorers. We believe that the best things in life are a little... bizarre.

Today, we carry over 5,000 collectible items and partner with ethical breeders to offer 200+ species of exotic pets, all backed by our health guarantee and lifetime care support.

What We Stand For

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity

Every collectible is verified and authenticated. We guarantee the legitimacy of every item we sell, with certificates of authenticity on high-value pieces.

Ethical Sourcing

All our pets come from responsible, licensed breeders. We never support wild capture and provide full health documentation for every animal.

Community First

We host monthly collector meetups, pet care workshops, and trading events. Our community is the heart of Bizarre Co.

Sustainability

Eco-friendly packaging, carbon-offset shipping, and partnerships with wildlife conservation organizations.

Expert Support

Our team includes certified herpetologists, avian specialists, and collectible appraisers ready to help you.

Lifetime Guarantee

30-day health guarantee on all pets and a satisfaction guarantee on all collectibles. Your trust matters.
