2. PRODUCT CARD IMAGES — a decision for you
   The eight cards use the merchant's own product photos straight from the CJ
   feed (img-us.aosomcdn.com, cdn11.bigcommerce.com) rather than /media/NN.
   That is why the cards work with no uploading.
   House style says product images are /media/NN. This departs from it on
   purpose, because a product feed supplies images for exactly this use.
   If you would rather host them yourself, download the eight images, upload
   them in Admin -> Media, and swap each src for /media/NN.

   Built to the house layout taken from your leopard-gecko-care-sheet.png,
   in the current plum/magenta rather than the old brown.

   No PDFs. Your free guides are images: serveGuide() reads the mime off the
   media row and the existing ones are .jpg. One image is both the thumbnail
   and the download, so there are no separate thumbnails to make either. The
   post's download cards now point at /img/<file> and /download/<file> the way
   your seed SQL does, with class="no-zoom" on the thumb.

   To register them:
     1. Admin -> Media -> upload both PNGs. Note the number each one gets.
     2. Open drafts/indoor-cat-setup-guide.guides.sql, replace
        <CARE_MEDIA_ID> and <CHECKLIST_MEDIA_ID> with those numbers.
     3. Run it against D1:
          npx wrangler d1 execute <your-db> --remote --file=guides.sql
        (or paste the two INSERTs into the D1 console)

   Both then appear on /free-guides automatically, and the cards in the post
   start working.

   Worth checking once: util.js has a wireDownloadCards() that runs over every
   post body before it renders. I have not seen that function, so load the post
   after publishing and confirm the two cards look right.

   Source for the sheets is in drafts/sheet-source/ — data-driven, so the text
   can be edited and re-rendered without redrawing anything.
